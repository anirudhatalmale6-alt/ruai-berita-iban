Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Introduction

Ruai Berita Iban ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Ruai Berita Iban mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

Information Collection

Our app is a news reader that fetches articles from the Ruai Berita Iban website. We do not require user registration or login. We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

Data We May Collect

  • Device Information: We may collect device type and operating system version for app performance optimization.
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you enable push notifications, we store your device token to send you news updates. You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.
  • Advertising: Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Data Storage

Bookmarked articles are stored locally on your device only. We do not upload or sync your bookmarks to any server.

Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (push notifications)
  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • WordPress REST API (news content)

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is a general news reader suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
